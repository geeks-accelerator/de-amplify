## Step 5: Tensions (internal only)

1. **Bullet 2 versus the body on whose number $1.4T is.** The summary bullet is scrupulous: "Meta says penalties could reach $1.4 trillion, states say $200 billion more likely." The body preserves this. **No internal contradiction**, and this is worth recording as a positive finding: Reuters is the only source in this batch that gets the attribution right in its own summary furniture.
2. **"Tens or hundreds of billions" (S2) versus $1.4 trillion (S4).** Not a contradiction, but the lede's range silently excludes the defendant's own estimate. A reader of the lede alone would not know a trillion-dollar figure is in play.
3. **The "allegedly" in R17 has an unresolved referent.** "Meta sought to block his testimony, allegedly for deleting Signal messages" leaves open whether the allegation is that Bejar deleted them or that this was Meta's stated ground. Material, because one reading impeaches the witness and the other does not.
4. **Six weeks (R21) is asserted flatly** with no source, in an article otherwise careful about attribution.

## Step 6: Synthesis

**S1. The magnitude in dispute has two numbers and each belongs to a different party.**
Meta's filing produces $1.4 trillion; the states told the court last week the figure is nearer
$200 billion. Neither is an award. *Basis: PARTY plus COURTROOM. Quality: the $200B is new to this
project's record and closes a question the MDL ledger left open.*

**S2. Liability is decided by a judge, not the eight jurors.**
The jury is advisory. Any headline of the form "jury finds Meta liable" would be wrong about this
proceeding in a way that matters. *Basis: RECORD.*

**S3. The remedy ask is a brake-design ask.**
Eliminating likes and infinite scroll, time limits for younger users, enforcement of the under-13
floor. This is the states asking a court to specify product controls. *Basis: RECORD via reporter.*

**S4. Both sides conceded ground in openings.**
Meta concedes some users struggle and that private language was loose; the states concede social
media benefits some people and disclaim any intent to destroy the company. *Basis: COURTROOM.*

**S5. The first witness is an insider on brake efficacy specifically.**
Bejar's testimony is about whether the safety tools worked, not about whether harm occurred, which
places it directly on this project's thesis. *Basis: COURTROOM.*
