## Step 5: Tensions (internal)

1. **The headline says "seek" and the body says "if the states win, they have said, they will seek".** The headline asserts a present demand; the body describes a conditional future one. This is the most consequential internal tension in the batch, because the headline is the part that propagates.
2. **"Damages" throughout, against a case pleaded on consumer-protection and privacy statutes.** Statutory civil penalties are not damages. Reuters says penalties. One of the two is imprecise, and the distinction decides whether the number is compensatory or multiplicative.
3. **N7 scales $200B against market cap; the Guardian scales the same figure against annual revenue; Reuters against three years of profit.** Within this document there is no contradiction, but the choice of denominator is an editorial act and is not flagged as one.
4. **N9 says "nearly $1 billion" for New Mexico while other sources in the batch say $567 million.** Internally consistent here, but it silently aggregates two phases.

## Step 6: Synthesis

**S1. The states' own number is roughly $200 billion, and it is conditional.**
Sourced to a court filing, framed as what they will seek if they win. *Basis: RECORD. Quality: the strongest sourcing for the $200B in the batch, and the clearest statement that it is contingent.*

**S2. The remedy ask is the fullest published list.**
Bans on infinite scroll, autoplay and like buttons for under-18s, plus beauty filters and engagement-optimized algorithms. *Basis: RECORD via reporter.* This is a court being asked to enumerate prohibited design features by name.

**S3. Two age lines run through one case.**
The data claim is about under-13s; the design remedy is about under-18s. *Basis: implicit, N27.* Not stated by any source, and it changes what a win would mean.

**S4. An appellate ruling on the speech defence landed this month.**
The Ninth Circuit is reported to have rejected Meta's motion to dismiss on First Amendment grounds. *Basis: RECORD via reporter, single-sourced in this batch. Quality: NEEDS-PRIMARY, and it is the highest-value unverified claim in the whole set.*

**S5. Litigation cost is now material on its own.**
Over $2 billion in a quarter against $18 billion quarterly profit. *Basis: PARTY.* Relevant to the paper's deterrence argument independent of any verdict.
