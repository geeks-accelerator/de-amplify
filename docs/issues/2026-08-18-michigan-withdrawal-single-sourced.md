# Michigan's departure from the state coalition rests on one local station quoting a spokesperson

Reported by a Detroit station quoting a spokesperson, and by nothing else in the corpus. The site
carries the coalition arithmetic that depends on it (more than 30 at filing, 29 at trial), so the
claim is load-bearing in a small way while resting on the weakest source class this project accepts.

**Deliberately not a Tension in the MDL ledger.** The ledger's Tensions own questions about that
record; this is a question about a party's membership that the docket may never state directly, and
no ledger claim asserts it.

## What was already checked, and why it proved nothing

Michigan is **absent from the Ninth Circuit caption** in No. 24-7032. That looked like corroboration
for about a minute and is not: the caption reflects the parties to an appeal **docketed in 2024**,
years before any 2026 withdrawal. It is a hit that is not confirmation, which is the corollary this
project already documents to the null-result rule.

Worth trying next: the Michigan Attorney General's own press releases, and any notice of dismissal
or substitution on the trial docket.
