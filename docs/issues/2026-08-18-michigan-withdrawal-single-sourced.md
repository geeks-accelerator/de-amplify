# Michigan's departure from the state coalition rests on one local station quoting a spokesperson

Reported by a Detroit station quoting a spokesperson, and by nothing else in the corpus. The site
carries the coalition arithmetic that depends on it (more than 30 at filing, 29 at trial), so the
claim is load-bearing in a small way while resting on the weakest source class this project accepts.

**Deliberately not a Tension in the MDL ledger.** The ledger's Tensions own questions about that
record; this is a question about a party's membership that the docket may never state directly, and
no ledger claim asserts it.

## What was already checked, and why it proved nothing

Michigan is **absent from the Ninth Circuit caption** in No. 24-7032. That looked like corroboration
for about a minute and is not: the caption reflects the parties to an appeal **docketed in 2024**,
years before any 2026 withdrawal. It is a hit that is not confirmation, which is the corollary this
project already documents to the null-result rule.

Worth trying next: the Michigan Attorney General's own press releases, and any notice of dismissal
or substitution on the trial docket.

## Closed 2026-08-20, and it turned out to be a question about the record after all

External research resolved this and, in doing so, **relocated it**. This was filed here because no
ledger claimed it. Once researched it plainly is a question about the MDL record, so it is now
**claim 17a of `docs/distillations/mdl-3047.md`**, and the boundary in this directory's README
worked as intended in both directions.

## What the research found

**Four states left, not one, and they left in January 2025**, not in 2026 as the coverage that
prompted this implied: **Michigan, Georgia, Missouri and North Dakota**. Michigan's Attorney
General's office attributes it to discovery burden, saying the court "approved most of Meta's
voluminous discovery demands upon the States and their agencies (such as MDHHS), demands with which
the State of Michigan could not possibly comply", with a filing estimating roughly 700,000
documents.

**That closes an arithmetic this project had been carrying on coverage alone.** 33 joint federal
filers in October 2023, minus these four, is the 29 at the August 2026 trial. The ledger previously
recorded 29 as what several outlets said, with a Tension noting no source explained the drop.

## The corroboration, and why it counts now when it did not before

Earlier in the day this issue recorded that Michigan is absent from the Ninth Circuit caption and
that **this proved nothing**, because the caption reflects an appeal docketed in 2024. That was
right. With a dated January 2025 withdrawal in hand, the same caption becomes **structural
corroboration**: it omits Michigan and Missouri entirely and lists North Dakota and Georgia as plain
"Plaintiffs", separated from the "Plaintiffs - Appellees" block. That is the pattern four pre-appeal
withdrawals would leave.

A hit is not confirmation. A hit plus an independent account, agreeing on a specific structure, is
evidence. The claim is tiered OBSERVED rather than ESTABLISHED because the withdrawal filings
themselves were not located, and that gap is now a Tension on the ledger rather than an issue here.
