# Meta's $2.40 billion quarterly legal charge is verified and has no argument to attach to

Verified against Meta's Form 10-Q: **$2.40 billion of charges related to legal proceedings** in the
quarter ended 2026-06-30.

**It is a genuinely useful datapoint with nowhere to live**, which is the definition of parked here.

Two things to get right if it is ever used. It is an **accrual**, the company's own estimate of what
it expects to lose, not money spent on defence, and the accurate version is worse for Meta than the
coverage that called it legal expenses. And it is **quarterly**, so whatever section carries it has
to be willing to refresh it every three months or it becomes a stale number on a site whose whole
proposition is that figures carry dates.
