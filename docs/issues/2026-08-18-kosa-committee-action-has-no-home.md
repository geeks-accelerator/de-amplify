# The Kids Online Safety Act cleared committee, is verified, and has nowhere to go

Verified against the congressional record: **ordered to be reported favorably by Senate Commerce on
2026-08-05**, with an amendment in the nature of a substitute.

**Parked rather than published, and the reasoning is the useful part.** The policy paper has no
section about federal bills, so adding this would be a stray fact wearing a citation. It is also
weak evidence on its own: most bills ordered reported die there, the reported text is a substitute
nobody here has read, and a bill's posture dates fast.

## Two corrections to carry if this is ever written up

The coverage that prompted the lookup said "last week" and "bipartisan". The action record dates it
to **August 5**, and **no vote tally appears in it**, so bipartisan is not available from that
source. The bill's 76 cross-party cosponsors are evidence about the bill, not about the committee
vote.
