# Whether India's IT Amendment Rules were notified after the May 2026 comment deadline is unknown

The ledger-candidates research pass confirmed the drafts were **unnotified** and recorded that the
status after the May 7, 2026 comment deadline is unknown.

Lifted out of that plan's open questions for the same reason as the Character.AI thread: a plan is a
dated record, not a standing queue, and this one has an answer that exists somewhere and simply has
not been fetched.

It matters more now than it did in July. Section 4 of the paper gained two comparative decisions on
2026-08-18, the French Conseil constitutionnel ruling and Australia's minimum-age duty, and the
argument they support is that **the workable instrument is calibrated, service-specific and
operator-facing**. A third jurisdiction either strengthens that or complicates it, and either is
worth knowing.

## Partly answered, and the part that is answered may be a different rule. 2026-08-20

**An IT amendment was notified.** MeitY notified the Information Technology (Intermediary Guidelines
and Digital Media Ethics Code) Amendment Rules, 2026 by Gazette Notification **G.S.R. 120(E) on 10
February 2026**, effective **20 February 2026**, following a public consultation opened on 22
October 2025.

**It is probably not the instrument this thread was about, and the dates are why.** This issue
inherited a question about drafts whose comment period closed on **7 May 2026**, three months
*after* the rules above were already notified. Two different amendment cycles is the simplest
reading, and treating them as one would be exactly the sort of same-name conflation this project
guards against elsewhere.

**It is also about a different subject.** The notified rules govern synthetically generated
information: provenance metadata, visible watermarks on AI-generated media, and a three-hour
takedown window for deepfakes. This project's interest in comparative regulation is minor
protection and feed design, which is what puts France and Australia in section 4 of the paper.
**A deepfake-provenance rule does not obviously belong beside them**, so even fully confirmed, this
may resolve to "notified, and not relevant here", which is a perfectly good outcome for an issue.

Two things to establish before this closes: whether the May 2026 draft is a separate instrument and
what became of it, and whether either touches minors or engagement design at all. Both want an
official MeitY or Gazette source rather than the law-firm commentary this pass found; note that
`meity.gov.in` returns a near-empty shell to a plain fetch, the same trap already documented for
the European Commission's press corner.
