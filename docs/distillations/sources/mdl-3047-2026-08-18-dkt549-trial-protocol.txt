 1          WHEREAS the Parties have met and conferred in light of the Court’s guidance about trial
 2   procedures at the August 13, 2026, pre-trial hearing and in Pre-Trial Order No. 8, ECF 534 (3392);
 3          NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties hereby stipulate and agree, subject to approval of the Court, that
 4   the following procedures shall govern during the first Multistate Attorneys General (AGs) trial starting in
 5   August 2026:

 7   I.     Trial Exhibits
 8          A.      Deadlines. The following deadlines apply to overall Exhibit Lists, the specifications for
                    which are set out in this Section.
 9
                    1.       Deadlines to Exchange Exhibits. On July 28, 2026, Meta sent its stamped
10                           exhibits to the AGs. On July 31, 2026, the AGs sent their exhibits to Meta. Both
                             Parties made these exchanges of exhibits subject to the reservation of rights
11                           outlined below, Section I.I, including an exception for exhibits to be used solely
                             for impeachment or rebuttal and demonstratives.
12
                    2.       Exhibit Lists. The parties filed exhibit lists on July 23, 2026, subject to the
13                           reservation of rights outlined below, Section I.I.
14                           a)     The parties were required to file a list of their 300 priority exhibits on July
                                    27, 2026 (the “Priority Exhibit List”). The parties may seek to admit into
15                                  evidence other exhibits from their Exhibit Lists that are not on their
                                    Priority Exhibit List. In addition to filing the exhibit lists, the parties shall
16                                  deliver an electronic version of all the exhibits to the Court on July 31,
                                    2026.
17
                             b)     During trial, the parties shall use witness binders on direct examination
18                                  and cross-examination and provide the Court with its own copy on a
                                    rolling basis. Exhibits should be contained within a binder with each
19                                  exhibit separated by a tabbed page denoting the exhibit number. Absent
                                    agreement otherwise, a party shall also provide copies of the binder and/or
20                                  all exhibits to be used with a witness on direct examination to the other
                                    party. A party must provide opposing counsel with a copy of any
21                                  document to be shown to the witness before the witness is shown the
                                    document. A party may use exhibits not included in the binder if the party
22                                  in good faith did not anticipate the use of the document at the time the
                                    binder was prepared, provided the party makes copies of the exhibit
23                                  available to the Court, witness, and the other party. For exhibits that
                                    cannot be printed (e.g., data compilations), the exhibit will be identified in
24                                  the binder by a slipsheet, and the party seeking to use that exhibit will
                                    provide to the other party and the Court an electronic or otherwise
25                                  accessible version. Counsel (and their agents) will not communicate with
                                    witnesses about the substance of the witness’s testimony or the trial at any
26                                  time after that witness has started to be examined by an adverse party until
                                    the witness is excused.

 1    B.    Numbering Exhibits for Identification. The AGs’ portion of the Exhibit List shall be
            1-9,999. Defendant’s portion shall be 10,000–19,999.
 2
      C.    Branding Exhibits for Identification. Each exhibit will be branded with a colored (i.e.,
 3          non-white) exhibit tag, modeled after the Court’s Trial Exhibit Tabs provided on the
            Court’s website, at the top right of the first page that identifies the unique exhibit number,
 4          or otherwise in a location that does not block the bates-number. Electronically generated
            exhibit tags shall be acceptable. The Court’s exhibit tags can be found at
 5          https://cand.uscourts.gov/sites/default/files/standing-orders/YGR-Trial-Exhibit-
            Tags.pd_.pdf.
 6
      D.    Exhibit Endorsements. Each party shall brand each page of their exhibits with an
 7          endorsement that identifies the unique exhibit number and page number of the exhibit.
            The page number shall maintain a constant length of four or five numeric digits
 8          (including zero-digit padding). The Exhibit Endorsement shall conform to the following
            format: [Exhibit Number]. (e.g., Ex. 0001.0001).
 9
      E.    Production Format for Exhibits.
10
            1.     PDF Format. All exhibits shall be properly branded and stickered with exhibit
11                 tags (as specified in this order), and individually produced in PDF format, to the
                   extent an exhibit can reasonably be converted to PDF format and remain usable.
12                 The file name of each PDF exhibit shall correspond to the unique exhibit number.
13          2.     OCR. All electronic trial exhibits exchanged and/or submitted to the Court in
                   PDF format shall be processed using optical character recognition (OCR) and
14                 shall be fully text-searchable at the time of exchange and/or submission.
15          3.     Native Format. If a document cannot reasonably be converted to PDF format
                   and remain usable (e.g., Excel file, audio, video, animation), then the exhibit shall
16                 be produced in native electronic format. The file name of a native file shall
                   correspond to the unique exhibit number (e.g., 000001.wav”). A slipsheet (PDF)
17                 for the native document shall also be provided that includes the Bates number,
                   confidentiality designation, and the trial exhibit sticker. The slipsheet shall state
18                 “Native Format Exhibit.”
19          4.     Document Size. The parties shall not alter or shrink original documents to
                   smaller than full page size unless such alteration preserves the integrity of the
20                 document and is only for ease of presentation at trial. The parties should note any
                   objections to alterations or shrinking of documents which fail to preserve the
21                 integrity of the document. Any party that intends to use an altered or shrunk
                   document pursuant to this provision shall provide as a numbered exhibit to the
22                 opposing party the full-size version of that document, and shall provide in the
                   exhibit list description a cross-reference to the shrunk/altered version.
23
            5.     Conferral. The Parties agree to confer in good faith on any concerns regarding
24                 the production format of exhibits and resolve any such concerns in advance of an
                   exhibit’s intended use at trial.
25

26          service list (if the production is sufficiently small) or via a secure File Transfer Protocol
            (FTP) site, with the exhibits organized in numerical order, in clearly labeled folders
27          stating the number range of exhibits contained in each, and in file sizes that can be easily
            downloaded on a personal computer). If a party produces its exhibits via production

15                                have stipulated that the document is a business or public record

            3.     Format of Lists. The parties will exchange exhibit lists in Excel format (see
 6                 I.G.2.a). The Parties will periodically submit updated versions of the filed
                   versions of the exhibit lists with the Court, formatted in accordance with the
 7                 Court’s Standing Order Re: Pretrial Instructions in Civil Cases, Appendix. They
                   will populate stipulations to admission (see I.G.2.g-h) in the “Stipulation to
 8                 Admit” column, will populate objections as of the submission in the “Objection”
                   column (see I.G.2.i), and will populate the date on which an exhibit has been
 9                 admitted in the “Date Admitted” column, in consultation with the courtroom
                   deputy (see I.G.2.k). The parties shall submit to the Court the exhibit list as to the
10                 Priority Exhibits no later noon, August 17, 2026.

11          4.     Static Exhibit Numbers. For tracking purposes, the parties shall maintain, and
                   not change, the unique exhibit numbers assigned to the exhibits listed on their
12                 Exhibit Lists. That will remain true even if a party subsequently withdraws an
                   exhibit from its list. In that event, the exhibits will not be renumbered nor will a
13                 different document be substituted in with that exhibit number.

14          5.     Audio / Video Evidence. For any significant amount of audio and/or audio-
                   visual evidence, including deposition designations played, the court reporter is
15                 relieved of their obligation to transcribe in real time. At the end of the business
                   day on which any audio and/or audio-visual evidence was completed, the Party
16                 calling the witness or admitting the exhibit shall provide the court reporter with a
                   transcript of what was played so that it can be attached to the transcript. The
17                 opposing Party shall be provided with an identical copy of anything provided to
                   the court reporter.
18
            6.     Exhibit Lists During Trial
19
                   a)     Exhibits admitted at trial shall be provided to the courtroom deputy on a
20                        rolling basis by close of court on the day admitted. They shall be placed
                          in binder and separately tabbed by exhibit numbers. This set shall be the
21                        official set which will be provided to the jurors.

22                        (1)     Each exhibit shall be pre-marked with an exhibit tag placed in the
                                  top right corner of the first page of a document. Parties are to use
23                                different colors than white for the exhibit tags.

24                        (2)     If an exhibit is a physical object (rather than a document), a picture
                                  should be taken and placed in the respective binders.
25
                   b)     No later than the start of closing arguments, the parties shall provide the
26                        Courtroom Deputy with a list of Admitted Exhibits for use by the jury in
                          its deliberations.

 1          7.     Potential Trial Exhibits That Do Not Need to Be Listed on the Exhibit Lists.
                   The parties agree that the following groups of documents need not be listed on
 2                 their Exhibit Lists. The parties reserve their rights to object to the admissibility of
                   these documents as trial exhibits on all other evidentiary grounds. If used at trial,
 3                 the parties will assign exhibit numbers to these materials for the record.
 4                 a)      Demonstratives: Demonstratives that parties intend to use at trial (which
                           are subject to other provisions).
 5
                   b)      Other: Exhibits used solely for impeachment or rebuttal.
 6
                   c)      Pleadings: Complaints and answers filed in this MDL.
 7
            8.     Exhibits To Be Delivered to Jury Room. Upon conclusion of the presentation
 8                 of evidence, the parties shall review the exhibits and confirm the accuracy of
                   those going into the jury room. The parties shall complete, deliver, and file a
 9                 certification with the Court confirming their review.
10    H.    Opening Slides. The parties agree to exchange opening slides in advance of their
            presentations at 3:30 PM PT the day before opening statements. The parties will confer
11          on any objections the evening before opening statements and raise any unresolved
            disputes to the Court the morning before opening statements are to begin. In the event
12          opening statements are scheduled to begin in the afternoon, the parties shall confer to
            reach agreement on a reasonable alternative exchange deadline. No party shall
13          incorporate the opening slides of the other into its own opening slides. Opening slides
            exchanged shall be numbered sequentially with numbering visible on the face of the
14          exchanged slides, and shall be in the same order the party intends to use during opening.
15    I.    Reservation of Rights
16          1.     The parties reserve their rights to object to the introduction and/or admissibility of
                   any document listed on any Exhibit List.
17
            2.     The parties agree that the inclusion of any exhibit on the Exhibit List does not
18                 waive or affect any prior confidentiality designation or any obligations concerning
                   such designations in this litigation.
19
            3.     The parties agree that the inclusion of a particular document on a party’s Exhibit
20                 List is not intended to be a waiver of that party’s right to object to the introduction
                   and/or admissibility of that document for any purpose.
21
            4.     The parties reserve their rights to modify, withdraw, or supplement in good faith
22                 their trial Exhibit Lists prior to and during trial.
23          5.     To the extent a party supplements their Exhibit List to add exhibits, that party
                   shall provide the newly added exhibits, consistent in form with the provisions laid
24                 out in Sections I, at the same time as the newly served Exhibit List. Any
                   supplementation, whether via email or FTP, will include (1) the numbers of the
25                 exhibits being supplemented; (2) the newly added exhibits; and (3) and updated
                   exhibit list identifying the exhibits being added (through highlighting or some
26                 other easily identifiable visual cue).
